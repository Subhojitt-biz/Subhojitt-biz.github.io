BizLens.

Exploring how AI and business analytics can turn everyday business data into practical insights.

01 / ABOUT

The idea

Data should help
businesses see clearly.

BizLens is an early-stage project by Subhojit Choudhary focused on building practical AI and analytics tools for business decision-making.

The broader concept explores workflows where businesses can provide information such as sales, stock movement and income data and receive organized analysis, Excel outputs and understandable business insights.
